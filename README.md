**実際には動かないぜ。魂で動かせ。🤘**

# Musicker

## ARE YOU FUCKIN' READY TO ROCK? 🤘

**Turn it up. Pick your poison. Hit PLAY.**

Rusty amps, jumping meters, and stupidly loud music.\
Musicker is a Windows music player built for one thing: enjoying HR/HM the way it deserves.

Forget the complicated stuff.\
Load up your music, hit PLAY, and you're good to go.

**LOUDER IS BETTER.**

---

## WHAT THE HELL IS MUSICKER?

Clean, shiny, modern music players are nice.\
But sometimes you just want to listen to music surrounded by rusty gear and beat-up amps.

Musicker is a local music player built for your own music collection,
with an interface unapologetically obsessed with HR/HM.

Load a music folder or an iTunes playlist,
pick a track, and enjoy your usual music with a slightly louder-looking interface.

### FEATURES

- Load tracks from your music folders
- Import iTunes playlists
- SHUFFLE / REPEAT playback
- Album artwork display
- Volume control with the VOLUME knob
- An interface built entirely around HR/HM
- No installation required

### ABOUT THE KNOBS

The **VOLUME** knob in the middle actually controls the volume.

The knobs on the left and right?\
**They're dummies.**

Turn them all you want.\
They won't add gain. They won't add distortion. They won't do a damn thing.

**They don't actually work. Turn 'em with your soul. 🤘**

## HOW TO USE

1. Open **PLAYLIST** in the upper-right corner.
2. Select **iTunesプレイリストを読み込む** (Load iTunes Playlist) or **音楽フォルダを読み込む** (Load Music Folder). These menu items are currently in Japanese.
3. Select a track and play it. Use **SHUFFLE** and **REPEAT** to change the playback mode.

Music files are not included.\
Use your own local music files.

To use an iTunes playlist, you will need your library XML file.
